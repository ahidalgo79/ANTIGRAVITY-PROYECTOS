"The objective of this Prueba de Redacción is to assess and improve writing skills by providing structured feedback on grammatical errors, spelling mistakes, and clarity in written communications. Participants should submit their redaction tests in a Microsoft Word document with double-spaced text, 12-point Times New Roman font, and one-inch margins. Feedback will focus on grammatical corrections, spelling errors, clarity in arguments or statements, and overall readability. Participants will receive detailed comments with specific examples where necessary."
